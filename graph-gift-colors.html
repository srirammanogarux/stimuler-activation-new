Graph + Gift · Color Guide

Every colour used by the growth graph, the gift transition and the two paywalls. Values are literal hex — copy them straight into Figma styles. The whole system is nine golds plus one indigo; nothing else is a brand colour.

01 · The gold ramp

Rule: gold is progress & reward only. The single interactive colour in this flow is indigo #6C63FF (offer pill, selected plan). Never use gold for a “tap me” affordance, and never use indigo for progress.
